Still at the desk at 81.

Allen has spent most of his life writing for children and for the people who teach them. The plays and workbooks grew out of years of watching what actually holds a class of ten-year-olds. The music is the other half of him.

He is putting the back catalogue online a piece at a time. Some of it is here now.
