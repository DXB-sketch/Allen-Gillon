A word about the author.

Allen has spent most of his life writing for children and for the people who teach them. The plays and the workbooks came from years of watching what actually holds a class of ten-year-olds. The music is the other half of him.

He is 81, still writing, and bringing the back catalogue to the stage a piece at a time.
