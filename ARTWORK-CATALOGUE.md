All 36 originals have a price; seven remain enquiry-only under the user’s instruction. Prices on those seven are guide prices, not an availability promise.

Titles follow source captions where supplied; otherwise they are descriptive labels. Dimensions and materials are included only where supported by the captions. Historical Facebook availability must be confirmed before live checkout, including paintings shown at the arts centre.

## Maintaining the gallery

Edit `content/artworks.mjs` for titles, prices and availability. Each image records its original source URL. `scripts/import-art.mjs` records the reviewed import mapping and can regenerate the catalogue from the original downloaded asset bundles; it will overwrite manual catalogue edits. Sold/commissioned works must remain enquiry-only until Allen confirms they are available. Database stock is separately tracked for sandbox and live; changing a catalogue flag does not automatically reset a sold item.
