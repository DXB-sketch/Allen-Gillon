Teacher for the NSW Department Of Education in 1964, spending almost 25
years as a Primary Teacher. During that time Mr Gillon completed additional
courses in musical arrangement at Sydney Conservatorium Of Music. He has
written and had published a number of classroom texts, currently in use in
Australian Schools. His musical training and experience resulted in his being
granted many play and drama assignments, including a huge production of
“Oliver” in the Penrith Department Region in 1977, co-ordinating 3 Primary
schools, a 250 piece choir and a 13 member orchestra. Mr Gillon’s love of

THREE HEROES OF SHERWOOD
The Play
ACT 1 Scene 1
Inside view of a lavish state room with throne.
The Sheriff of Nottingham receives a report from Sir Blackheart about
progress by the Poison Ivy Legions. They have the task of clearing the forests
where Robin Hood and his merry men live. The dastardly destroyers have
already cleared William Tell out of his forest and now they are preparing new
broadaxes for the forest around Robin Hood.
ACT 1 Scene 2
The Poison Ivy Legion appears in front of Curtains singing their theme song
“Poison Ivy”. The Choir joins them.
ACT 2 Scene 1
Inside view of a Spacecraft, with a backdrop looking from space to a section
of planet Earth below.
The Curtains opens to reveal Buck Rogers and his crew orbiting and
approaching mother Earth. They have come from the 21* century and realise
through their viewers, that there’s trouble afoot in Sherwood forest, still in
the 14" century. They decide to land in a clearing and help the two heroes in
distress.
ACT 3 Scene 1
In Sherwood Forest, around a campfire
Robin Hood and merry men are discussing how they might repel the Legions
of Sheriff Rogue. William Tell and family are also present, offering help to
Robin and Little John. Suddenly they see a spacecraft arriving.
ACT 3 Scene 2
Buck Rogers and crew introduce themselves, offering to use their future
technology to right the wrongs.
ACT 4
Buck, Robin and William Tell capture the Poison Ivy Legion and apply the
Thought Inducing helmets to them. Sheriff Rogue and Sir Blackheart suddenly
arrive to inspect progress and too are captured by the 3 Heroes.
Buck and crew havetoleavetocatch theTimeWarp.
