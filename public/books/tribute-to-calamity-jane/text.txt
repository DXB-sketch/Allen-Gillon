CALAMITY JANE
OVERVIEW
FUN IN THE WILD, WILD WEST!
When the school teacher quits and leaves town, the folks of Dakota decide to
bring in a new one. There’s only one track into town and that is through
Dakota Pass. The Chief of the Rap-dancin’ Tribe seizes on the opportunity to
make some easy money. They hold-up the stage coach and capture the new
school teacher. Awesome! What happens next? They should have listened to
their tribal elders ...

the new school teacher. Song: Medley of songs from Calamity Jane plus Line
Dancing and Rap.
