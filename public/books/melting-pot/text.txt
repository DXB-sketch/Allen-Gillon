OVERVIEW
MULTICULTURAL
This fun play raises some of the problems associated with inter-racial acceptance.
Good overcomes bad, when the local Rescue Squad (made up of a number of
Ethnic persons) is called in to rescue the two main characters. They are injured,
hanging over a cliff, after stealing and crashing the new Chinese Judo instructor’s
car.
INVOLVEMENT
Safety equipment is required for Scenes 1 and 8, so I suggest you involve the local
Rescue Squad or borrow from them such things as ropes, ladders, flashing lights,
stretchers etc.
Scene 3 includes a Chinese Fable being read by Doctor Loo Chong, I suggest you
involve Kindergarten children to perform as eight chimes of the Xylophone.
Scenes 7 and 9, The International Children’s Concert, requires many national
dances and songs. I suggest you involve some of the local ethnic groups (if
appropriate), to assist the children in the concert.
SONGS
Scene 2 opens with Choir singing songs like “Everybody Loves Saturday Night!” in
various languages. This may require local ethnic representatives to supervise choir’s
pronunciation.
Scene 4 requires Connie to solo a song about brotherly love. An appropriate song
may be substituted according to local popularity.
