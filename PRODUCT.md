# PRODUCT.md — allengillon.com

## Register
brand (portfolio + storefront: the design is the product)

## What this is
A single portfolio-and-store site for Allen Gillon: composer, playwright, and author.
It replaces an outdated Wix music site and consolidates three bodies of work into one
place that is easy for Allen to maintain. Domain: allengillon.com.

## The person
Allen Gillon, 81. A lifetime across three creative outputs:
- **Music** — jazz standards, covers, and original compositions, studio-recorded.
  Historically shared free to stream on the old site. Free streaming stays.
- **School plays** — scripts written for primary-school performers, roughly ages 10 to 13,
  for classroom and school-production use.
- **Books** — two shelves. (1) Classroom communication-skills books, three so far,
  designed to be taught from. (2) Children's storybooks, some released years ago as
  YouTube "audiobooks" using early robotic text-to-speech that now reads as dated and
  needs re-recording.

## Users, in priority order
1. **Teachers, drama and English coordinators.** Time-poor. They scan to judge fit fast:
   what age band, how many parts, how long, how is it used in class, can I get it now.
   They are the commercial engine (plays + communication books). The site must let them
   assess and acquire with no friction.
2. **Parents and young readers.** Browsing children's books. Warmth, clarity, reading
   level, and whether audio exists all matter.
3. **Music listeners,** general and local. Want to press play and hear it, free, no account.
4. **Allen himself,** 81. Will update the site and show it to peers. It must feel dignified
   and be genuinely operable by him.

## Product purpose (two jobs, equal weight)
- **Portfolio:** present a lifetime of work with the seriousness it deserves.
- **Store:** sell books and any paid scripts through one cart; music streams free.

## Tone
Grounded, plain-spoken, a little dry. A veteran maker who does not need to sell hard.
Short sentences. Use Allen's real specifics (titles, ages, cast sizes, years) instead of
atmosphere. Not whimsical, not corporate, not "storyteller-brand" precious.

## Accessibility is a brand value, not compliance
Older author, older visitors, teachers on school hardware. Large readable type, strong
contrast, obvious tap targets, nothing important reachable only by hover, no faint
grey-on-pale text. This is part of the identity.

## Anti-references (these make it fail)
The generic "AI portfolio" fingerprint, banned from a prior audit of an earlier mockup:
- Uppercase letter-spaced eyebrow/kicker labels stacked over section headings.
- Warm cream or sand background + characterful display serif + warm metallic accent
  (the default cluster). Do not use the Fraunces + neutral-grotesk pairing.
- A single recolored italic "accent word" dropped inside a big serif headline.
- Solid pill button beside a ghost/text link, each with a hover-sliding arrow.
- The same eyebrow / heading / muted-aside module opening every section.
- Reflexive hover choreography (underline wipes, lift-on-hover, sliding arrows) on generic `ease`.
- Decorative glassmorphism or backdrop blur.
- Gradient text, side-stripe borders, identical icon-heading-text card grids, hero-metric blocks.
- Em dashes in copy. Use commas, colons, periods, or parentheses.

First-order category reflexes to avoid outright:
- Music portfolio, so reach for vinyl / turntable + warm analog palette. No.
- Children's books, so reach for crayon scribbles and primary-colour clip-art. No.
- Education, so reach for cheerful rounded blue. No.

Concept "not-this":
- Not a music-first site. Music is one of three, and arguably not the lead.
- Not a startup landing page. No "Get started" energy, no feature grids.
- Not childish because children are the subject. The buyers are adults and teachers.

## Strategic principles
- **Three doorways, one house.** Music, Plays, Books each get a clear path. The homepage
  routes; it does not dump everything at once.
- **Teacher-first metadata.** Plays and communication books surface age band, cast or group
  size, run time or length, and classroom use before anything decorative.
- **Free to hear, buy to keep.** Music streams free on the page; books and any paid scripts
  go through one shared cart.
- **Placeholder-friendly.** The structure must look right and dignified with placeholder
  covers, sample-only scripts, and "recording coming" notes, because not everything is
  digitized yet.
