Sixty years of things written to be read aloud.

Allen Gillon writes school plays for young performers, communication-skills books for the classroom, and children's stories. He also records jazz. The scripts and books are here to buy or licence; the music is here to play, free.

Allen Gillon

Allen has spent most of his life writing for children and for the people who teach them. The plays came out of years of watching what actually holds a class of ten-year-olds; the communication books came out of the same place. The music is the other half of him.

He is 81, still writing, and putting the back catalogue online a piece at a time. Some of it is here now. The rest is on its way.

Bribie Island, Queensland · writing since the 1960s
