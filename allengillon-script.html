NOTE. ON THE AUTHOR

Allen has spent most of his life writing for children and for the people who teach them. The plays and the workbooks grew out of years of watching what actually holds a class of ten-year-olds. The music is the other half of him.

He is 81, still writing, and putting the back catalogue online a piece at a time.

Bribie Island, Queensland · writing since the 1960s
